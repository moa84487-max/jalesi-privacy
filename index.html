Privacy Policy for JALEESI

JALEESI is an app for organizing and tracking your reading and for reading your own books (PDF, EPUB and text files). We respect your privacy. This policy explains what happens to your data.

What data do we collect?

We do not collect personal data about you. There is no account and no sign-in, and the app does not send your books, notes or reading history to the developer or to any server. The only information that leaves your device is the anonymous usage statistics described under “Ads, statistics and updates” below.

What is stored on your device?

  • Your library: book titles, authors, covers, ratings, notes, quotes and summaries.
  • Your reading progress, statistics, goals and settings.
  • Files you add yourself (books, tones and sounds).

This data stays on your device only. Uninstalling the app, or using “Delete everything” in Settings, removes it.

Ads, statistics and updates

Ads. The app contains no ad network and does not track your identity. Ads in JALEESI are not served by an automated ad network: we choose each advertiser ourselves, and the ads are the same for every user and are not targeted using any data about you. The Android app may show a banner on the Library, Sounds and Statistics screens from an advertiser we work with directly (an image and a link, clearly marked “Ad”). The image and the list of ads are loaded from our own GitHub Pages site, so the advertiser does not receive any data from your device. If you tap the ad, its link opens in your browser and that website’s own policy applies. We do not send any information about you with it.

Statistics. By default (and you can switch this off any time in Settings), the installed app uses Google Firebase Analytics to collect anonymous usage statistics, such as: app opens, books added and finished, reader opens, cards shared, the app language, and device type, OS version and country as collected by Firebase. We do not collect your book titles, their content or any personal data, and we turn off the advertising ID. Google processes this data under its own policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy . If you switch it off, no statistics are collected. If we ever add anything else that collects data, we will update this policy and let you know beforehand.

To tell you about new versions, the installed app may occasionally read a small public file (version.json) from our GitHub Pages site. The app sends no information about you with it, but GitHub sees your IP address as with any web request.

Permissions the app asks for

  • Notifications and exact alarms: to remind you at your reading times and to alert you when the timer ends.
  • Run reminders after the phone restarts: to reschedule your reminders.
  • Keep the screen on and vibration: while reading and when an alert fires.
  • Internet: to check for new versions, to send the anonymous statistics, and to load partner banners.
  • File access: only when you pick a file yourself, or when you save or share a card image.

The app works offline. Sharing through your phone's share sheet is your own choice, and the app you pick is the one that receives the image.

Web version

The web version of JALEESI may use Google Analytics for anonymous visit statistics. This does not apply to the Android app.

Children

JALEESI is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone, including children. The anonymous statistics described above contain no personal information.

Changes to this policy

We may update this page. The “last updated” date at the top will change when we do.
